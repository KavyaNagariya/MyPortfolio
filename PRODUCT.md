# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters and hiring managers looking to evaluate backend and AI skills for open roles.

## Product Purpose
To serve as a professional portfolio that showcases projects, skills, and bio to secure roles in Backend and AI engineering.

## Positioning
Positions Kavya as a pragmatic, build-first Backend/AI engineer ("plan first, let the agent work") who ships real products.

## Operating Context
Viewed primarily in web browsers by recruiters and engineering managers, requiring fast scanning of skills, bio, and projects.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Built with React, Vite, and Vanilla CSS.
- Currently uses placeholder boxes for Projects and Favourites, awaiting final content.
- Must preserve the established dark-mode design, typography, and masonry/grid layout structures.

## Brand Commitments
- **Name:** Kavya Nagariya
- **Roles:** Software Developer • Backend AI Engineer • AI Builder
- **Voice:** Pragmatic, direct, build-focused ("Backend guy. Python and FastAPI first").
- **Contact:** Preserved specific links for GitHub, LinkedIn, Email, X, and Buy Me A Coffee.

## Evidence on Hand
- Confirmed bio bullet points and professional links.
- Missing final project details and favourite media assets (using placeholders).

## Product Principles
1. **Developer-centric appeal:** The vibe and messaging should resonate strongly with technical recruiters and engineering managers.
2. **Scannability:** Information must be easily digestible within seconds.
3. **Pragmatism:** The design should let the actual work and "build-first" mindset speak for itself without unnecessary fluff.
